## 📜 The Pensieve — About Me

> *"It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities."*

I like understanding how things work — and how they break. I'm a **CS + Applied Math undergrad at Adani University**, building a foundation in algorithms, data structures, and Python, and now moving toward **machine learning and generative AI**.

I was selected as one of **40 students nationwide** for the **ACM Summer School on Competitive Programming at DAU (DA-IICT)**, where I worked on graph theory, dynamic programming, and greedy strategies through daily CodeChef contests — the closest thing to dueling practice a Muggle CS student gets.

My applied math background gives me a solid grip on discrete math and the reasoning behind algorithms, which I want to carry into AI — how LLMs reason, how retrieval-augmented generation works, and how to evaluate models for hallucination and reliability.

- 🪄 **Currently exploring:** Python for ML · LLMs · RAG · multimodal AI · trustworthy AI
- 🔮 **Open to:** research exposure, workshops, and collaborations in GenAI
- ⚡ Competitive programmer — graph theory, DP, and greedy algorithms under time pressure
